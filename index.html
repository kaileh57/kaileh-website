Research

Amortized Opacity Marginalization Improves C/O Interval Calibration for Brown-Dwarf Retrievals

Kellen Heraty · Sculptor AI · arXiv preprint, September 2026

I train neural posterior estimators to account for uncertain molecular opacities in brown-dwarf spectra. On perturbed simulated spectra, opacity marginalization improves nominal 90% C/O interval coverage from 61.4% to 89.4% in matched three-seed ensembles.

Readable is not causal: decoy representations that decode but do not drive behavior

Kellen Heraty · Research writeup and experiments

Controlled experiments on Gemma 3 compare what probes can decode with what activation patching and steering show a model uses. The study tests task-relevant and decoy information with matched interventions, held-out controls, and checks of the model’s own explanations.

Fixed positive sampling is nearly saturated

Kellen Heraty · ARC White-Box Estimation Challenge, Phase 1 · August 2026

A sampling-based estimator for ReLU networks, with a certified bound for its unpruned Kerdock sampling core. The writeup separates the mathematical guarantee from the adaptive pruning used in my challenge submission.
