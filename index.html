Research

Amortized Opacity Marginalization Improves C/O Interval Calibration for Brown-Dwarf Retrievals

Kellen Heraty · Sculptor AI · arXiv preprint, September 2026

I train neural posterior estimators to account for uncertain molecular opacities in brown-dwarf spectra. On perturbed simulated spectra, opacity marginalization improves nominal 90% C/O interval coverage from 61.4% to 89.4% in matched three-seed ensembles.
