Clean Energy Transition

Lead your nation through the challenges of transitioning to clean energy while balancing economic, social, and environmental priorities. Make strategic investments, enact powerful policies, and respond to unexpected events to achieve your climate goals by 2050.

How to Play

Clean Energy Transition is a strategic decision-making game where you take on the role of Energy Minister for a nation starting in 2025.

Through a series of decisions over 5 turns (25 years), you must:

  • Invest in clean energy technologies and infrastructure
  • Implement policies that guide your nation's transition
  • Respond to unexpected events and crises
  • Balance your budget, public approval, grid stability, and emissions reduction goals

Each nation has unique starting conditions, strengths, and challenges. Victory depends on meeting your climate commitments while maintaining a strong economy and public support.
